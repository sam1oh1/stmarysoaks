Can you help to monitor the health of oak saplings in the area?

Young oaks face many risks: deer browsing, bark stripping by squirrels, increasing stress through climate change related drought and flood periods.

Below is a map of young oak trees nearby. Help by finding them and photographing them, to build up a photo archive and monitor their health. To submit photos, see the instructions below.

How to Help

Submitting photos using the Epicollect5 app

We use the free Epicollect5 app to collect photos and location data for each sapling. Follow the steps below — it only takes a few minutes to set up.

1

Download the Epicollect5 app

Epicollect5 is a free data-collection app available on iOS and Android. You can also use it in a web browser.

App Store (iOS) Google Play (Android)
2

Add the Oak Sapling Recorder project

In the app, tap + Add Project (top right, as shown below) and search for Oak Sapling Recorder.

3

Find a sapling on the map

Use the interactive map above to find an oak sapling near you. Once you've located a tree, open the app, tap Oak Sapling Recorder, then tap Add Entry.

Tip: Save the map for offline use if you expect poor mobile signal on location.
4

Fill in the survey form

📍 Location — the app captures your GPS position automatically.

📷 Photo — take a clear photo of the sapling, ideally showing the whole tree.

📝 Notes — any observations about the tree's condition (e.g. healthy, browsed by deer, damaged).

5

Upload your entry

After you have saved your entry, click the UPLOAD button (as shown below), followed by:

UPLOAD DATA
UPLOAD PHOTOS

Thank you! Every entry counts — even a single photo once a year helps track each tree's progress over time.
